On Stage & On Record

Speaking

I thrive on the stage, sharing ideas that connect enterprise design, product design, and digital innovation. My talks dive into the future of AI, security, and compliance, while showing how design thinking, product thinking, and strategy turn complex challenges into opportunities.

I often draw on methods like Working Backwards to help leaders reimagine products with clarity and customer focus. Whether it's shaping enterprise strategy or unlocking creativity, I bring energy, clarity, and actionable insights that inspire teams to design with impact and trust.
